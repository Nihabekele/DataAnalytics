term. Click the plus sign to add a search for Hamburger (Food).

100
What is the lowest dip?
61
b) What happened to the line for Milkshake? What do the high and low look like now?
The milkshake line is the bottom blue line. After adding hamburger, it appears almost flat. Its high and low are both 0 on the rescaled chart.
 
5. Why did the line for Milkshake change? Hint: Click on the icon next to “Interest over time” for more information about how trend results are displayed.
The milkshake line changed because Google Trends rescales all terms relative to the highest search volume. Adding hamburger made milkshake look very small compared to hamburger so its line dropped near the bottom of the chart.

6. Add a third term to your search, this time for Breakup (Topic).
a) Do you see any patterns that suggest a relationship between searches for Breakup
and Hamburger?
Breakup searches stay at 0 while Hamburger is very high so there is no relationship between them.
b) How about between Breakup and Milkshake?
Both Breakup and Milkshake stay at 0 all the time, so there is no pattern or relationship between them.

7. What does all of this suggest about the reliability of the conclusions reached in the
article?
Since Breakup and Milkshake stay at 0, the chart doesn’t show real patterns, so the conclusions about them may not be reliable.
